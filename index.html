How I work

The same six steps on every project, from the first stakeholder conversation to the recommendation.

  1. QuestionAgree what decision the business needs to make.
  2. RequirementsElicit needs and write them up as a BRD with user stories.
  3. DataSource, clean and validate it before trusting it.
  4. AnalysisQuery and model in SQL and Excel to find the driver.
  5. DashboardBuild it in Tableau or Power BI so others can explore.
  6. RecommendationSummarise what to do next, ranked by impact.
